# AegisBank AI — Customer Verification Policy
Policy ID: CVP-2026-04
Category: Verification
Version: 1.0

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how a customer's identity is verified during a fraud investigation.

## 2. When Verification Is Required
Verification is required before releasing, blocking or reversing any held transaction, and whenever a new beneficiary receives a large transfer.

## 3. Verification Steps
The analyst confirms the customer's identity using at least two independent factors from the customer's verified record, such as registered phone, national identity details and recent transaction history. Details supplied by the person making the suspicious transaction must not be used.

## 4. Beneficiary Checks
A first-time beneficiary receiving a transfer far above the customer's normal amount must be confirmed with the customer before the transfer is treated as legitimate.

## 5. Record Keeping
The outcome of every verification attempt, including failures, is recorded against the case.
