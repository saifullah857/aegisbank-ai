# AegisBank AI — Card Fraud Investigation Policy
Policy ID: CFI-2026-05
Category: Card Fraud
Version: 1.0

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the investigation of suspected card fraud, including stolen cards, card testing and unauthorised online purchases.

## 2. Stolen Card Indicators
Unauthorised purchases from an unfamiliar location, several rapid purchases in minutes, or purchases in a merchant category the customer never uses are indicators of a stolen or cloned card.

## 3. Card Testing
Many small transactions in quick succession, often followed by a large purchase, indicate card testing. The velocity indicator and the unusual merchant category indicator together are the strongest signals.

## 4. Online Purchase Review
Online purchases from a new device and an unusual location are reviewed against the customer's normal merchants. Repeated failed attempts followed by a success are escalated.

## 5. Customer Liability and Disputes
Customers are not held liable for transactions confirmed as fraud. Disputed transactions are documented in the case and closed only after a human analyst records the final decision.
